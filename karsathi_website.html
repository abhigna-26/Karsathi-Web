The Problem

Small businesses deserve better tools.

India's 6 crore MSMEs run their business on WhatsApp and notebooks. Existing software is built for accountants, not business owners.

📱

Fragmented workflow

Tally for accounting. ClearTax for GST. Khatabook for ledger. Three apps, three logins, zero integration — and you still forget deadlines.

Penalties from confusion

GST filing missed because the due date slipped. MSME vendor payment delayed past 45 days — now non-deductible. Small mistakes, large penalties.

Basic compliance questions answered for free by your CA on WhatsApp — but only when they're available. You run a business 24/7. Your support should too.
